## course description
Duccio Rocchini [born 1975 in Siena] has been Full Professor at the Alma Mater Studiorum University of Bologna since December 2019, after having been Associate Professor at the University of Trento since February 2017. He obtained his PhD in 2005, on the topic of biodiversity estimation using remote sensing imagery.
